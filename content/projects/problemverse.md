## Overview

Great solutions start with clearly defined problems. **ProblemVerse** provides a collaborative space where students, innovators, and communities document real-world challenges, brainstorm solutions, and connect with technical builders.

## Proposed AI Architecture & Capabilities

- **Automated Problem Classification:** Tagging and categorizing problems (e.g. environment, healthcare, education, logistics) through NLP text classifiers.
- **Semantic Duplicate Detection:** Identifying related or duplicate problems across different posters to unify problem-solving momentum.
- **Contextual Solution Recommender (Planned):** Mapping existing open-source tools and proven approaches to newly submitted issues.
- **Team & Collaborator Matching (Planned):** Connecting developers possessing the relevant skills to problems matching their interests.
