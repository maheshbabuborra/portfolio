## Overview

Students and early-career developers frequently struggle with interview preparation because generic question collections rarely match targeted roles and difficulty levels. The **AI Interview Assistant** is an intelligent preparation platform built to generate tailored technical, conceptual, and behavioral questions.

## Features

- **Role & Track Customization:** Select specific engineering domains such as Machine Learning Engineer, Backend Developer, or Data Analyst.
- **Difficulty Gradients:** Dynamically scale questioning depth from core fundamentals to complex scenario-based inquiries.
- **Structured Preparation Guidance:** Delivers tips on essential concepts, trade-offs, and expected answering frameworks.
- **Answer Evaluation (Prototype / Planned):** Providing contextual feedback on candidate answer responses.

## Practical Value

Provides candidates with a structured, personalized, and repeatable mock preparation workflow without requiring expensive coaching tools.
