## Overview

Reading through lengthy research papers, syllabi, and technical reference PDFs consumes excessive study time. The **AI Notes & Document Summarizer** extracts document text and leverages natural language processing to produce structured, digestible study summaries.

## Core Capabilities

- **Multi-Format Document Parsing:** Upload PDF or text study materials directly into the application.
- **Key Takeaways & Concept Extraction:** Highlights critical definitions, formulas, and structural takeaways.
- **Executive Summaries:** Provides hierarchical overviews for quick conceptual revision before tests or meetings.
- **Study Quiz Generation (Future Enhancement):** Planned capability to generate self-testing quizzes based directly on uploaded notes.
