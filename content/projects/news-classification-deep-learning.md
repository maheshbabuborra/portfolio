## Overview

Organizing vast streams of digital news and articles manually is an impractical task. This project implements an end-to-end Deep Learning and Natural Language Processing workflow to categorize news articles into distinct topics automatically.

## Concepts & Architecture

The project explores fundamental and advanced concepts in Deep Learning applied to NLP:
- **Text Preprocessing & Tokenization:** Cleaning raw text, stripping stop words, and converting words to sequences.
- **Vectorization & Embeddings:** Transforming textual sequences into numerical representations suitable for neural networks.
- **Deep Neural Network Architecture:** Designing Dense multi-layer neural architectures with non-linear activation functions (ReLU, Softmax).
- **Training & Evaluation:** Utilizing categorical cross-entropy loss, backpropagation, and Adam/SGD optimizers with validation monitoring to mitigate overfitting.

## Dataset & Experiments

- Conducted experiments utilizing the **Reuters news dataset** consisting of multiclass news stories.
- Analyzed the trade-offs between vocabulary constraints, sequence lengths, and model convergence.
- Visualized training vs. validation loss curves to inspect generalization performance.
