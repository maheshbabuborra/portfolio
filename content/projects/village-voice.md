## Overview

**Village Voice** is an AI-powered complaint management platform designed to bridge the gap between residents of villages or municipalities and local administrators. Traditional public grievance systems are frequently slow, manual, difficult to track, and lack structured prioritization. 

Village Voice modernizes this process by allowing citizens to lodge issues with descriptions, photographs, and location context while leveraging Machine Learning to categorize and organize complaints automatically.

## The Problem

Residents regularly encounter issues such as:
- Damaged roads and potholes
- Broken street lights and electrical hazards
- Drainage blockages and sanitation bottlenecks
- Drinking water supply interruptions

Traditional grievance methods require manual triage by administrative staff, resulting in lost complaints, misdirected department routing, duplicate entries, and delayed response times.

## The AI / ML Solution

Village Voice integrates Artificial Intelligence to streamline the lifecycle of a complaint:
- **Automatic Complaint Categorization:** Text classification models evaluate user grievance descriptions to categorize them into relevant civic domains (roads, electricity, sanitation, water supply).
- **Automated Department Routing:** Routes the issue directly to the relevant municipal department.
- **Duplicate Complaint Detection (Planned):** Semantic similarity matching to group multiple reports of the same incident together.
- **Priority & Urgency Prediction (Prototype):** Identifying urgent hazards (such as exposed live wires or collapsed drainage) to elevate resolution priority.
- **Administrator Analytics:** Visual dashboards summarizing complaint density, resolution times, and frequent community bottlenecks.

## Key Features & Highlights

- Designed an intuitive, citizen-focused grievance reporting workflow.
- Built NLP-driven text categorization for rapid complaint triage.
- Support for attaching photo context and location details.
- Structured administrative dashboard for resolution tracking and department accountability.
- Emphasizes practical digital governance and civic accessibility.
